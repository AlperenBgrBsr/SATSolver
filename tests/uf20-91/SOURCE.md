# uf20-91

1000 uniform random 3-SAT instances, 20 variables and 91 clauses each
(clause/variable ratio 4.55). All are satisfiable.

Obtained from **SATLIB — Benchmark Problems**, maintained by Holger H. Hoos and
Thomas Stützle:

<https://www.cs.ubc.ca/~hoos/SATLIB/benchm.html>

These instances are redistributed here unmodified, for convenience, so that
`make test` works from a fresh clone. They are not part of this project's source
and are not covered by its MIT licence.
